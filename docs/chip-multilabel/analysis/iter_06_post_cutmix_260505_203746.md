## 3. Eight candidate directions — graded

Each candidate is rated against (a) does it directly attack bb+scratch /
fork+scratch / bb-fork-overfire? (b) optimistic vs pessimistic Δ macro_f1
estimate, (c) bb+sr recall risk, (d) novelty / paper citation.

### #1 ★ CE+LS=0.20 + CutMix soft-target (KL on softmax)

The single change: keep T1's CE+LS=0.20 base, layer CutMix on top, but mix
labels via **softmax soft-target KL** instead of BCE. Concretely the
augmented chip's target becomes `λ·one_hot(y_a) + (1-λ)·one_hot(y_b)`
softmaxed with the same LS=0.20 smoothing, and loss is KL(softmax(logits) ||
soft_target).

- **Why it attacks the residual**: T1's per-class F1 on the bb+scratch /
  fork+scratch combos was already strong on the *single* sides (bb F1 0.97,
  fork F1 0.93 in T1). The 78/52 collapses appeared because T1 had no combo
  training signal at all (multi-class CE forces sum-to-one, can't represent
  two positives). Adding CutMix puts two-positive supervision back, while
  KL soft-target preserves CE's softmax-induced runner-up sharpness — the
  exact property that BCE lost.
- **Why this beats T7c**: T7c uses BCE so its single-class precision is
  0.88 fork, 0.93 bb (vs T1: ~0.97 / ~0.95). The single-class AP for fork
  on T7c is 0.755 — the rate-limit. KL soft-target keeps CE single-class
  AP high while picking up CutMix's combo gain.
- **Optimistic** (CutMix gain stacks on T1 base): macro_f1 **0.945-0.955**
  with bb+sr recall **0.85-0.92** (cutmix paste teaches combo, KL keeps
  fork sharper than BCE).
- **Pessimistic** (KL soft-target on small data may underfit): macro_f1
  **0.918-0.925**, bb+sr recall **0.55-0.70**.
- **Risk**: KL+CutMix on 4-class softmax with LS=0.20 needs care — the
  smoothed soft target has min mass 0.05 per class which is itself a
  combo-mimicking signal. May not blend cleanly with CutMix mass split.
- **Citations**: Yun et al. CutMix (1905.04899); Müller et al. "When Does
  Label Smoothing Help?" (NeurIPS 2019, on KL+LS).
- **Atomic spec**: identical to T7c run config except `loss=ce_ls_kl`,
  `ls=0.20`, `cutmix=0.5`, `cutmix_target=soft_kl`. One run, ~10 min.

### #2 BCE LS sweep (0.10, 0.15, 0.25) at fixed cutmix=0.5

- **Why**: T7c jumped LS from CE-optimal 0.20 straight into BCE without
  re-finding BCE's optimum. BCE's effective gradient magnitude is different
  (per-class independent), so LS optimum likely differs. The CE LS curve
  was monotonic 0.05→0.20 then drop, sharp peak. BCE may peak at 0.10 or
  0.15.
- **Direct attack on residuals**: indirect. Lower LS would sharpen fork
  logits (helping fork's AP=0.755), but might re-introduce fork over-fire.
- **Optimistic**: 0.10 hits BCE peak → macro_f1 **0.935-0.940**.
- **Pessimistic**: T7c LS=0.20 was already the BCE peak → −0.005 to flat.
- **Cost**: 3 trains × 10 min = 30 min. **Not atomic in spirit** — sweep
  rather than one change.
- **Citation**: Müller et al. 2019.

### #3 cutmix-rect sweep (0.25, 0.75) at fixed p=0.5

- **Why**: rect=0.5 (T7c default) cuts a half-area square. Smaller (0.25)
  preserves more of the original chip → less label noise but weaker combo
  supervision; larger (0.75) → stronger combo signal but a 0.75-area paste
  may erase nearly the whole chip.
- **Direct attack**: rect=0.75 might force more bb+scratch CutMix pairs
  to retain both signals (since the "remaining" 25% of the original chip
  is small enough that the pasted 75% dominates label assignment).
- **Optimistic**: rect=0.75 → +0.005 to +0.010.
- **Pessimistic**: rect=0.75 erases too much → −0.01.
- **Cost**: 2 trains. Not atomic in spirit.
- **Citation**: Yun et al. CutMix (1905.04899).

### #4 ★ Class-balanced CutMix (force bb+sr / bb+scratch / fork+scratch pair sampling)

- **Why**: T7c's CutMix pair sampling is uniform over the 4 classes →
  bb-scratch CutMix pair occurs 1/16 of CutMix events. The 78 bb+scratch
  collapses reflect insufficient bb+scratch combo exposure during training.
  Class-balanced pair sampling would up-weight bb+scratch and fork+scratch
  pairs to e.g. 1/6 each.
- **Direct attack**: hits exactly the bb+scratch collapse (78 cases) and
  fork+scratch collapse (52 cases) — the two largest residuals.
- **Optimistic** (the 78+52 cases drop ~50%): macro_f1 **0.940-0.950**
  with bb+sr recall held at 0.96.
- **Pessimistic** (combo over-exposure causes single-class regression):
  macro_f1 **0.920-0.928**, bb+sr recall 0.96 (sr combo unaffected).
- **Risk**: requires pair-aware sampler implementation. Not a hparam flip.
- **Citation**: Akhter et al. "A Label Propagation Strategy for CutMix in
  Multi-Label Remote Sensing Image Classification" (arXiv 2405.13451) —
  most directly applicable. Wang et al. "Distribution-Balanced Loss for
  Multi-Label Classification in Long-Tailed Datasets" (ECCV 2020).
- **Atomic spec**: change CutMix pair sampler from uniform → forced pair
  weights `{(bb,scratch): 1/6, (fork,scratch): 1/6, (bb,fork): 1/6,
  (bb,sr): 1/6, (fork,sr): 1/6, (scratch,sr): 1/6}` — equal across all
  6 unordered pairs. **One change, one run.**

### #5 ASL + CutMix (γ_pos=0, γ_neg=2) on top of T7c

- **Why**: ASL with γ_neg=2 down-weights well-classified negatives, lighter
  than the γ_neg=4 that lost in iter 4 (T4 macro_f1 0.78). With CutMix
  providing combo supervision and γ_neg=2 dampening fork's noise-floor
  over-fire, both fork over-fire (58 bb→bb+fork) and bb+scratch / fork+scratch
  collapses (78+52) might improve.
- **Direct attack**: targets fork over-fire on bb single (58 cases). Less
  direct on the bb+scratch / fork+scratch collapses.
- **Optimistic**: macro_f1 **0.935-0.942**.
- **Pessimistic**: ASL γ=2 still too aggressive on small 327-chip data
  (T4 lesson) → 0.90 or below.
- **Citation**: Ridnik et al. "Asymmetric Loss for Multi-Label
  Classification" (ICCV 2021).

### #6 Stochastic depth (drop_path_rate=0.05) on top of T7c

- **Why**: anomaly-detection BKM directive ("docs/summary.md:159-172"
  recommends 0.05). Adds layer-level dropout regularization; may reduce
  fork over-fire (residual #3, 58 cases) and tighten fork AP.
- **Direct attack**: weak / generic regularization, no specific lever on
  bb+scratch or fork+scratch collapses.
- **Optimistic**: +0.005-0.010 from regularization.
- **Pessimistic**: F1/F2 already showed anomaly-detection BKM transfers
  poorly to chip multi-label (warmup −0.109, EMA −0.089). Stochastic depth
  could be similar.
- **Citation**: Huang et al. "Deep Networks with Stochastic Depth" (ECCV
  2016). Listed in iter 5 BKM table at F1=0.9985.

### #7 Lower LR (5e-5) for BCE on top of T7c

- **Why**: BCE's per-class independent gradients differ in magnitude from
  CE's softmax-coupled gradients. T7c reused T1's LR=1e-4 which is CE-tuned.
  BCE may want lower LR.
- **Direct attack**: indirect — slower convergence may give cutmix paste
  combos more steady supervision.
- **Optimistic**: +0.005.
- **Pessimistic**: 5e-5 underfits in 8 epochs → −0.02 to −0.05.

### #8 Drop bb+fork over-fire via I12 inference variant (no retrain)

- **Why**: 58 bb→bb+fork over-fires are inference-decoded. An I12 variant
  that requires fork sigmoid above bb sigmoid by margin m before declaring
  bb+fork could cap them. No retrain.
- **Direct attack**: only 58/2200 = 2.6% of total errors. Even cutting all
  58, macro_f1 gain is at most +0.005.
- **Optimistic**: +0.005.
- **Pessimistic**: pair gating creates new false negatives on real bb+fork
  (which T7c handles 150/160 = 94%) → flat or negative.
- **Citation**: none specific — band-aid like I11 was in iter 6.

## 5. Recommendation

**#1: CE+LS=0.20 + CutMix p=0.5 with KL soft-target.**

Reasoning: T1 → T7a was a −0.0691 macro_f1 hit purely from CE→BCE.
T7a → T7c was a +0.0694 cutmix recovery, but the recovery is on top of the
weaker BCE base. **The decomposition implies CE base + CutMix combo signal
is strictly dominant** *if* CutMix's two-positive supervision can be
expressed in CE. KL on softmax soft-target is exactly that: it preserves
CE's runner-up sharpness (the property that gives T1 its high single-class
AP) while reading CutMix's λ-mixed two-class target.

This is the only candidate that simultaneously addresses all three residual
modes (bb+scratch collapse, fork+scratch collapse, bb→bb+fork over-fire)
because all three are downstream of "BCE single-class precision is lower
than CE single-class precision." Restoring CE precision while keeping
combo supervision attacks the root.

bb+sr recall risk: medium. CutMix p=0.5 already trained the bb+sr pair via
visual co-occurrence on T7c (recall 0.962). The same paste mechanism with
soft-target KL should preserve bb+sr ≥ 0.85 (constraint floor) — soft-target
mass on the bb+sr pair is identical to BCE's pair mass at λ=0.5.

**Single atomic change**: T7c → T8 by replacing
`loss = bce_with_logits + cutmix_bce_target` with
`loss = kl_div(log_softmax(logits), label_smooth(soft_target_lambda_mix, eps=0.20))`.
All other hparams (epochs=8, batch=8, accum=4, LR=1e-4, cutmix p=0.5,
cutmix-rect=0.5, RandomAffine translate/scale aug, no rotation/flip, no TTA)
held identical to T7c.

Expected macro_f1 **0.94 ± 0.015** with bb+sr recall **0.85-0.92**.
