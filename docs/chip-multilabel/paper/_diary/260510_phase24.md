## Reframing the §6.17 thesis

§6.17 was written around the iter-37 finding "asymmetric
label is the missing 4th diversity axis". That claim is now
**reframed**:

- KD (axis 3, §6.16) and asymmetric (axis 4, §6.17 original)
  are **alternative diversity sources**, each delivering a
  4-bag ≈ 0.9961 / 0.9976 — real lifts over iter-30's hard-
  only 0.9945, but **not necessary** for the global optimum.
- The new pure-hard 4-bag adds a **g = 2 LS = 0.30 seed
  cell** (24_LS030 from iter 24) to three iter-26 cells,
  producing a (g, LS, seed)-spread basis spanning g ∈ {2,
  3, 4} × LS ∈ {0.30, 0.40, 0.50, 0.75}. This basis is
  richer than any iter-30 / iter-34 / iter-37 cell pool used.
- Pure-asymmetric 4-bag (37 A / D / E / H) reaches only
  0.9913; pure-KD 4-bag (33 A–D) reaches only 0.9873. Both
  single-axis bags lose to mixed bags. The KD and asymmetric
  axes are **marginal** diversity sources — they require
  hard-label cells in the bag to clear v15 ≥ 0.99.
