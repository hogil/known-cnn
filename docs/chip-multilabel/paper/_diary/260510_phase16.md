## Key paper findings (logged into §5.21 / §6.16 / §7.5)

1. **KD-student is a non-correlated diversity axis.** Replacing
   the weakest hard-label cell with iter-33 A KD-student lifts
   bag v15 bit-F1 + 0.0016 at zero cost change. The KD-student
   is trained on 14-bag teacher *probabilities* (α = 0.3,
   T = 4); its decision boundary is shaped by teacher consensus
   uncertainty and is qualitatively distinct from any hard-
   label cell.
2. **2-bag 26 B + 33 A at OR-mode = 0.9969** — the highest
   single number in the sweep. Frame as the **minimum-cost
   production-affordable bag**: 2× cost, 0.9969 / 1.25 % FAR
   vs 4× / 0.9961 / 0.00 %.
3. **Pure-KD 4-bag collapses to 0.9873** — KD-students are
   mutually correlated (all from the same 14-bag teacher), so
   bag-of-KD is rank ≈ 1 along the KD axis. This confirms
   KD's role is as a *new axis* against the hard-label basis,
   not as self-replacing scaling.
4. **Diversity > quantity reaffirmed at the KD-augmented
   level.** 5-bag (0.9929) and 6-bag (0.9961) do not surpass
   the iter-34 4-bag (0.9961); the KD-student is the missing
   fourth axis.
5. **The 0.9969 number from earlier internal discussions =
   2-bag 26 B + 33 A at OR mode**, NOT a 5-bag. Recorded
   canonically here.
