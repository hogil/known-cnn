## Findings

1. **Seed-fragility at the headline cell.** Re-training
   (g, A, B) = (3, 1.0, 0.5) with seeds 7 and 42 FAILs both at
   100 % `ni_FAR`. Single-model bit-F1 actually rises on the
   FAIL seeds (0.9834 / 0.9841 vs 0.9604 at PASS) — bit-F1 is
   not a leading indicator for dual-pass survival.
2. **Single-point sweet spot in (A, B)-space.** At g = 2 with
   seed = 1 fixed, both (1.0, 0.4) and (1.0, 0.6) FAIL. The
   PASS at (1.0, 0.5) is a knife-edge balance point, not a
   convex basin.
3. **Four-axis fragility.** §6.6's narrow-PASS-basin
   hypothesis was originally articulated in (g, LS)-space.
   Iter 38 extends it: PASS = function of (g, A, B, seed)
   with all four axes brittle at the headline cell.
