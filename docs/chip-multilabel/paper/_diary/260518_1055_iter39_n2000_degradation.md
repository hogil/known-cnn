# 260518 10:55 — iter39 4-bag paper-main n=2000 degradation (PAPER HEADLINE REVOCATION)

## Finding

Re-evaluation of the prior PAPER MAIN headline 4-bag majority-vote
ensemble at the production-grade `v15direct_n2000` (POS9 strict, OOD
strict 4-class) reveals a catastrophic degradation vs the n=200/n=500
headlines that anchored the paper.

```

|---------------------|---------------------|---------|---------|-----------|---------------------------------------------------------------------|
| v15direct n=200     |              0.9955 |   0.00% |   0.00% |     0.00% | past paper headline                                                 |
```

- `-0.0400 bit_F1` and `+4.05 pp Total FAR` going from n=200 → n=2000.
- NI-FAR and OOD-FAR both jump (4.10% / 3.91%), so the degradation is
  not concentrated on a single negative-class subset — it is the OOD
  weakness of the base members surfacing at the larger n.
- Composition: `{24_LS030_seed42 + 26B + 26D + 26H}` pure-hard majority
  vote `>= 2/4`.

## Root cause

`24_LS030_seed42` is a `g=2 LS=0.30` recipe whose OOD false-positive
rate is small on the n=200 sampler but large on the n=2000 chip
distribution. With only three other voters in the bag — one of which
(26H white-fill) also leaks under OOD strength-band breakage — the
2/4 majority cannot suppress the OOD false-actives. The bag's
per-bit majority cancel mechanism reaches its budget limit, and the
ensemble degrades to single-model-plus-correlated-error territory.

## Paper-narrative consequences

1. **PAPER HEADLINE REVOCATION.** The 0.9955 (n=200) / 0.9953 (n=500)
   iter39 4-bag pure-hard majority vote is no longer the paper's
   SOTA number. It is a sample-size over-fit artifact under the
   n=200 sampler.
2. **New PAPER HEADLINE** = chain v7 `vote_majority_bits {iter116J_s1
   + s77 + KD_v7} I10 = 0.9941 / 0.00%` at n=2000.
3. **New single-model SOTA** = `iter116J_s1 I10 = 0.9927 / 0.00%` at
   n=2000.
4. **Base-only no-KD ensemble headline** = `{s1+s77+s33_v15}
   vote_majority_bits = 0.9929 / 0.27%` at n=2000 — isolates the KD
   contribution on top of pure base-member diversity.
5. **Protocol note.** `n=2000` POS9 strict + 4-class strict OOD is
   the only production-grade reliable protocol. n=200 systematically
   under-reports OOD-weak failure modes of recipes whose diversity
   does not span OOD-robust corners (the `g=2 LS=0.30` family).

## Methodological insight (§6 saturation chapter material)

In the saturated regime (bit_F1 > 0.99), the cell that distinguishes
"real generalization" from "small-n over-fit" lives on the OOD-FAR
axis, not on the bit_F1 axis. A recipe can score 0.9955 at n=200
because the 200-chip sampler happens to miss the OOD-leak chips; at
n=2000 the same 4-bag has enough OOD exposure that its 4-vote ceiling
on the leak cells is exceeded — **not** a calibration shift but a
true OOD recall failure of the underlying base members. Recipes that
remain at 0.9941 / 0% across the n=200→n=2000 transition (the
iter116J family) are the ones that should anchor the paper's
production-grade SOTA claim.
