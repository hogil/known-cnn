## Asymmetric AB at g = 2 is non-monotonic

PASS: (1.0, 0.5), (0.75, 1.0).
FAIL: (1.0, 0.75), (0.5, 1.0).

The PASS region is **not the convex hull of {0.5, 0.75, 1.0}
edges** — small label perturbations trigger or break the FAR
floor depending on the first-position policy. Combined with
the iter-36 3-band symmetric-LS PASS pattern, paper §6
(narrow-PASS-basin hypothesis) now has rich evidence: the
PASS region of label space is non-monotonic in *both* the
symmetric LS axis and the asymmetric AB axis.
